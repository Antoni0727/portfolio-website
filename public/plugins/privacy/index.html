Privacy

Updated 16 November 2026

No ads, no tracking, no analytics and no cookies from this site. I only learn who you are if you get a plugin or email me.

What your browser keeps

A few small settings are saved in your own browser so the site remembers them: light or dark mode (for this visit only), which home page layout you saw, and your layout vote. They stay on your device. You can remove them any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

The layout vote

If you press “I like this one” on the home page, two things are sent so I can count the votes: the layout you picked and a random code made by your browser (so a changed vote counts once, and your browser keeps that code only after you vote). No name, no email, and your IP address isn't saved with it. Votes are stored on this site's host (below) and deleted once a layout is chosen.

Getting a plugin

Checkout and downloads, paid and free, are handled by Gumroad. Nothing is loaded from Gumroad until you press Buy or Get: then Gumroad's checkout opens and what you type there (your email, and payment details for paid plugins) goes to Gumroad, under their privacy policy. Gumroad is the seller of record, so your receipt, any VAT and refunds go through them. I never see your card details. Gumroad shares your email and what you got with me, as the creator. I use it only for your order: delivering the files and helping you if something doesn't work. I never sell it or pass it on.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Netlify. Like any web host, it handles your IP address and browser details to send you the pages and keep the site safe. The fonts are served from this site, not from Google.

Update check in the plugins

While TJ Quick Paste's Settings window is open, it reads one small file from this site to see if a newer version is out. It sends nothing about you or your computer: no ID, no cookies. Netlify sees that request like any page visit.

TJ Quick Paste on your computer

The plugin has no account, no tracking and no analytics, and it never sends anything about you, your projects or your files to me. It reads your clipboard only when you press its key. Pasted files go to the folder you pick; its settings and your last 20 pastes stay on your computer.

A link you paste is fetched straight from that site, like your browser would, so the site sees a normal visit from your computer. Logos are looked up by name on Wikidata and Wikimedia Commons. Screenshots from a link use a hidden copy of Chrome, Edge or Brave with its own profile, kept only on your computer. It never opens or reads your own browser's profile.

My Browser takes the picture in your own browser. With the TJ Quick Paste browser button (an add-on you load into Chrome, Edge, Brave or Arc yourself, from a folder on your computer), Quick Paste opens the link in a tab there, the button takes a sharp picture, hands it to Quick Paste on your computer and closes the tab. You allow that once. The button can't connect to anything but your own computer, and it doesn't read your browser's cookies, passwords, history or other tabs. Without the button, My Browser takes a picture of your browser window as your screen shows it.

For video links, the free tools yt-dlp, ffmpeg and Deno are downloaded the first time you need them, from their makers' releases (ffmpeg for Mac from ffmpeg.martin-riedl.de), and each one is checked against its published checksum before it's used.

Videos and links

Where a page has a video, it comes from YouTube's privacy-enhanced mode and nothing is loaded from YouTube until you press play. Links to YouTube, X, Instagram and TikTok take you to those sites, where their own privacy policies apply.

Your rights

You can ask what I have about you, ask me to correct it, or to delete it. Email tonyjedits@gmail.com and I'll answer within 30 days. If you're in the EU or UK, you can also complain to your local data protection authority.
