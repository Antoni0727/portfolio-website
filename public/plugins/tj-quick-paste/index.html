More than pictures

Paste a website

Copy a link and pick a sharp screenshot, the whole page or its main picture. Light or dark, see-through if you like, even of pages you’re signed in to.

Just the part you need

YouTube, TikTok, Instagram, X and more. Pick the part and the quality, or take just its sound as an MP3. Only download what you have the right to use.

Fix it before it lands

Edit Picture crops, removes the background, trims what’s left, blurs what’s private or enhances a small picture on the way in. It fixes picture clips already on your timeline too. Your original file is never changed.

The same look every time

Select a styled clip and turn on Same look: new pictures get its size, position and effects. The Quick Paste list shows what you copied with a pen on every picture, plus your last 20 pastes. Same look needs Resolve 21.1.2 or newer.

And everything else you copy

Files

Images, video and audio from Finder or Explorer, several at once. Video keeps its audio. WebP, AVIF, HEIC and WebM are converted.

Text becomes a title

Copy a line of text and it lands at the playhead as a Text+ or any title you pick.

GIFs, stickers and emoji

They come in as moving clips, see-through, Giphy and Tenor links too. One emoji becomes a big emoji picture.

Posts

A post on X, Bluesky or Threads comes in as a picture, or its video when it has one.

PDFs

Tick the pages you want: each one comes in as a sharp picture.

Fusion page too

Images become MediaIn nodes and text a Text+ node, merged in.

Signed-in pages

Sheets, Gmail, your dashboards: add the browser button once and My Browser takes the page as you see it.

Names that make sense

Web images are named after where they came from and messy names get cleaned up. Your own files keep theirs, and a file pasted twice isn’t copied twice.

Works with AI

AI assistants like Claude get a guide: ask for pictures, they find and paste them. Resolve Studio only.

Install

Then find it in Workspace › Scripts › TJ Quick Paste.

  1. Unzip the download.
  2. Double-click Install (Mac) or Install (Windows).
  3. Restart DaVinci Resolve.
  4. Give it a key in DaVinci Resolve › Keyboard Customization: click All Commands, search Quick Paste and press ⌥⌘V (Ctrl+Alt+V on Windows). Light: search Paste to Media Pool.

“Install (Mac).command” Not Opened? Click Done. Open System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway, then Open Anyway again and enter your password. Only the first time.

“Windows protected your PC”? More info › Run anyway.

Pro: DaVinci Resolve Studio 19 or newer. Light: Studio, or free Resolve 19 to 21.0 (its Settings window needs Studio). Mac and Windows. Text to titles needs 20.1, Effects and Same look 21.1.2. The AI guide needs Studio. Background removal and Enhance run on Apple Silicon Macs and 64-bit Windows; Apple’s own cut-out needs macOS 14. On a Mac, text pastes ask once for Accessibility, and My Browser once for Screen Recording. The free helpers (video downloader, ffmpeg, AI models) download the first time you use them.
