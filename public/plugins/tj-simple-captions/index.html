TJ Simple Captions

Word-by-word captions with a box that fits.

A caption title that reveals words one by one, with a background box that resizes to the text by itself. Works with AutoSubs.

Download free

DaVinci Resolve 19 or newer, Free or Studio.

What it does

Word by word
Each word appears as it’s spoken.
Box that fits
The background box resizes to every line by itself, so there’s nothing to adjust by hand.
Works with AutoSubs
Set it as your template and generate captions as usual.

Install

Then find it in Effects › Toolbox › Titles › Tony J Edits.

Watch the tutorial
  1. Open DaVinci Resolve.
  2. Double-click the .drfx file and click Install.
  3. Restart Resolve and find it in the Effects library.
