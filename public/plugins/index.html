  • Tools: unzip, double-click the installer for Mac or Windows, and restart Resolve. You’ll find them under Workspace › Scripts. The project template: Project Manager › Import Project. Free effects: double-click the .drfx file with Resolve open and click Install.

  • The effects and the project template work in every version. The tools (Quick Paste, Macros, Color by Bin) work in Resolve Studio, and in free Resolve up to version 21.0. The free 21.1 update blocks scripts like these, so on free Resolve stay on 21.0 to use them.

  • DaVinci Resolve 19 or newer, on Mac or Windows (free Resolve: 19 to 21.0 for the tools). Turning copied text into titles in TJ Quick Paste needs 20.1.

  • TJ Quick Paste Light is free and covers the basics. Pro adds everything else, listed in the Pro vs Light table above. Everything else on this site is free.

  • One person, on all of your own computers, for personal and client work. Please don’t share the files.

  • Yes, free updates for life. When there’s a new version, download it again from the same Gumroad link or your Gumroad library.
